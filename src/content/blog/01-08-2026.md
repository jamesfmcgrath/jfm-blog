## Consolidating My Website World

So I have a lot of domains. Several hobby sites. A couple of business sites. A blog. Domains spread across registrars. Hosting on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/). Others on [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/). Most were gathering digital dust and not updated regularly. The blog was on [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) and I was tired of the updates. [Drupal](https://drupal.org/) is my daily driver for professional projects so I like to branch out and play with other CMS platforms when I have the chance. Would love to use [Drupal CMS](https://new.drupal.org/drupal-cms) more but hosting can be a pain and it is overkill for all of these sites.

What do I do? Leave it be. Wrestle with trying to remember what goes where? Finding passwords, tracking subscriptions, trying to remember where I was the last time I touched the code. It made my brain hurt. I needed to consolidate. To bring peace and harmony to my digital playground.

I needed to start somewhere. Organising the domains seems like a good idea. What did I own? Where were they registered? When would they expire? My first task was gather all this information. From there I set the goal to bring all the domains under a single registrar. Rather than do that all at once I decided to move them as they came up for renewal. I went with [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Affordable. Easy to use. Lots of options for growth. The occasional outage for excitement. So many of my clients use the it felt like a good way for me to share in their world and learn some new tricks. Decision made. As each domain renews it moves there. Life is a little easier.

Hosting? I liked using Vercel for my [React](https://react.dev/) projects. Solid platform and had no issues. I will use it again. I had the Wordpress on Hostinger. I am cheap and they have been providing reliable service at a good price. So I decided to use up the three slots I had there. For everything else I chose [Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com/). Free. Easy to use. Smooth deploymentsI don't get a lot of traffic on these sites. If one blows up and gets a following I figured it they would have the options to meet my needs.

Code. No more Wordpress or custom [React SPAs](https://reactrouter.com/how-to/spa). Kind of. I had been playing with Astro and it does everything I need to do for the sites I work on. Easy to spin up. Fast. Secure. I write everything in markdown in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) anyway so no big leap for blog content. Let the migration began.

Migrating Wordpress to [Astro](https://astro.build/) took awhile. I used [Claude Design](https://claude.com/product/design) for a new simple look. Easy. [Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) did the migration. Took longer than I thought but did a good job. Been messing with [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) again as they gave me a $10 credit when I tried to cancel. Had it clean up the frontend. Solid work. In fact it was so good I had it migrate three React SPAs and a static React blog I was messing with to Astro. Knocked those out of the park. Was really happy with how it handled the work. It's actually churning through another site I remembered only this morning as I write. Will probably be done by the time I finish writing. It cranked out one this morning as I walking around a beautiful lake in [Sligo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sligo).

Bish. Bosh. Bang. Sorted. Have a lovely digital day.
